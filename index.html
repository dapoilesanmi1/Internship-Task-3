Academic story

From a Nigerian boarding school to a computer science engineering degree in Hungary.

  1. Schooling in Nigeria

    Went to a Baptist school in Nigeria from childhood all the way through secondary school, attending a boarding secondary school.

  2. Top of my set

    Graduated with one of the best grades in my set at the end of my secondary school and received an award for it.

  3. Choosing tech

    I have always wanted to work in tech because I'm enraptured by the science behind it and also notable characters in them.

  4. Why Debrecen

    I chose the University of Debrecen partly because it has housed relatives of mine before. I researched its tech centre and came away really impressed, particularly with its facilities, staffs, and academic programs.

    Final-year student in Computer Science Engineering, skilled in front-end development, particularly HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
